WHAT IS ASKED (STATUS "The count as a knob core ..."): a burst, and a burst chopped 16 words + 1 cycle, meet two long holds of the VRAM
at a line's start with one 16-word unit written between them; SyncMode 1 blocks of 16-32 take the time of the first hold only. With
the request "FIFO empty" and 35 words held ahead of VRAM both cannot be. This cuts each kind of transfer after n words and reads
back what the GPU stored: fill (the words stored, in order), pre (in place), and GPUSTAT at the cut.

THE THREE CORES (tools/hwrunner.js --emu, the SCPH-5501's BIOS; node experiments/knot/abortsum.js <out>; every row's start has the
line-edge poll's jitter of a few cycles, so a row is a draw and the DIGEST is the prediction):
  M        the count (experiments/intake/_mkcnt.js: a unit written as GPU_FILL_WB writes a block; line start 85 + 60 ticks, one unit
           between; refill 50) - what the bursts' gap lists fit
  M_noF2   M without the line start's second burst - what SyncMode 1's totals fit
  v319     v0.377.319
No core holds pixels back: each writes a word to VRAM as its intake takes it, so K 5 reads n - fill = 0 on all three. ON THE CONSOLE
K 5 decides what "fill" is: 0 = GP1(01h) lets the buffered words be written (fill is the intake's count, as here); 13..29 = it drops
them (fill is the WRITER's count, which stands still through each hold and steps 16 between them - then read K 0-4 as the writer).

WHAT EACH OUTCOME MEANS
  K 3 (SyncMode 1, 16): M cuts 6 of 147 rows with the intake behind (4-7 words left in the FIFO, GPUSTAT 00h: no request) - the blocks
      that end inside the second hold - and M_noF2 one, v319 none. The console's totals say none or one. If the console shows them
      as M does and still takes M_noF2's time (t at n 480: M 885-913, M_noF2 868-896), the request is not "FIFO empty" in a
      transfer's data; if it shows none, the second hold gives way to these blocks (or they are held elsewhere).
  K 4 (SyncMode 1, 32): every core LOSES WORDS here (a 32-word block meets a stopped intake: 16 fit the FIFO); hwtests/dma-len says a
      console's blocks of 32 never lose one. No loss on the console = its request waits for room the cores do not model.
  K 2 (chopped 16 + 4): M loses a lap at the line start, M_noF2 and v319 nothing; hwtests/gpu-a0chop says the console loses nothing
      at this pace. A blind feeder at 0.75 words a cycle that rides the line start says the second hold is not there for it either.
  K 1 (chopped 16 + 1) and K 0 (a burst): the stops of gpu-burstrow2 again, with the FIFO's words visible at each cut: n - fill grows
      to 16 while the intake stands, then the lap is lost.
  GPUSTAT at the cut: 12h (bits 28 and 25) with the FIFO empty; 00h while words wait in it. A row with fill < n and 12h would be the
      request up over a FIFO that is not empty.
