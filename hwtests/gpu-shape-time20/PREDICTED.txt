little (flat 0.25 cycle), and only the walk BEYOND 4 pixels a row costs a pixel's worth. So, TR less TL (bit 26):

  semi     walks 1 / 2 / 4: TR = TL within a few cycles (gpu-shape-time6's 64x16: TR = TL, 942)
           8 / 16: grows by ~0.49 a pixel beyond 4 (gpu-shape-time6's 320x16: 125)
