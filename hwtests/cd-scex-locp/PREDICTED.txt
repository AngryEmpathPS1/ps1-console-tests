WHAT THE MODEL SAYS: after the hold's kick (Q 63 -> 55) the last header the DECINT handler saw ($01DF) stays 60 until the new
track's 56 at ~78 ms - sector 61 completes inside the 2.9 ms after the jump when the handler is shut ($41.2), and nothing follows
it, so no header past the target is ever compared. $6B = 0Dh at ~35 ms in both rows; $49 clears at once in D0 (the ReadN), at
~37 ms in D3. Q never drops below 55: no second jump.
WHAT WOULD SAY THE CONSOLE TAKES ANOTHER PATH: in D0, $01DF stepping 60 -> 61 -> 62 (or 63) after the kick and before Q drops to
~47; or $6B / $49 at other times than these; in D3 the same with no jump. Whichever byte first differs is where the drive body
(src/cdservo.js) feeds the program wrong.
