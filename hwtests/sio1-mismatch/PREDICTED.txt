sio1-mismatch - PREDICTED before its first console run (2026-10-19), this emulator under hwtests/run.js --loopback-auto.
The two receivers this emulator has had disagree on R2 (the receiver twice as fast: the 8251A's 66h then E6h from the
next falling edge, the old per-frame model 66h alone) and R4 (four times slower: FFh against FEh). RM (BAUD rewritten
inside the frame) reads 55h under both - a console reading otherwise follows the new rate mid-frame. RB's queued bytes
take the new BAUD at their own start (the transmitter's rate per byte), so the burst arrives whole under both.
