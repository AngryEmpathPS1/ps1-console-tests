WHAT WOULD SAY WHAT.
- THE DISPLAY OFF. This core holds the VRAM for the scan-out whatever GP1(03h) says: D 1 = D 0. If the console's D 1 rows of the
  burst store every word (as its blanking rows do), the holds are the scan-out's and GP1(03h) stops them: the model needs that
  (one line). If they lose words as with the display on, the scan-out goes on fetching a blanked display - or the holds are not
  fetches. If they lose FEWER (one hold of two, or shorter), part of the line start is not the display's: refresh.
- THE LINE. The console's rows that store every word between rows that lose 16-32 should be the ones with ln in the blanking
  (about 240 of 263 lines show); if they are spread over visible lines, they are something else and gpu-a0abort3's reading of
  them is wrong.
- BETWEEN A BURST AND 16 + 1. This core serves a fetch in idle slots a 6.5-cycle piece fits: 64 + 1 and 32 + 1 leave the port
  6 cycles in most units and read as the burst (31-32 of 41 rows short), 16 + 2 reads between (21), 8 + 1 loses nothing. If the
  console's 32 + 1 reads as 16 + 1 rather than as the burst, it is the PAUSE in the words that lets the scan-out in, not the
  port's idle time.
- 16 + 1 AT THREE STARTS: the first word lost moves 16 words with the start if a unit's phase against the first hold's end
  decides it (gpu-a0abort3's rows at 307 / 323).
