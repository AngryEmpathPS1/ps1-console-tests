
gpu/gp0-store
The CPU behind its own GP0 stores (cached; Timer 2 cycles, fastest..slowest of 16 over the refresh).
A n: lhu T2; n NOP stores to GP0; lhu T2.  B k: 3 NOP stores, k nops.  C: 3 NOP stores, a GPUSTAT read.
R: a 1x1 rectangle (3 words).  RS: and a GPUSTAT read.  T k: an 8-bit 16x16 textured rectangle reloading its
CLUT, k nops.  TC: its CLUT cached. There are no assertions - compare against your emulator.
