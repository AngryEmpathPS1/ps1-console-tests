
cdrom/cd-boot-read
The BIOS's first double-speed read: SeekL from single speed, Setmode 80h at its INT2, ReadN at the Setmode's
INT3 - the first INT1 from the ReadN's write, and GetlocL then (the first sector's header). Cycles at the system
clock (1 ms = 33,869). About a minute; nothing prints until the end.
