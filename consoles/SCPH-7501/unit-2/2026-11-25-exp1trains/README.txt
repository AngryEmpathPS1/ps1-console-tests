hwtests/exp1-trains on the SCPH-7501-u2 (BIOS 4.1, GameShark 2.4 carrying 61016b99) through the new Pico (3460, fw 2p-feedfix17), 2026-11-25, tools/exp1trains.js. 21 trains of 128 stores to 1F060008h (the cart's comms-out register), unrolled, from RAM, cold pass then a marked warm pass, at the kernel's EXP1 / COM delay values. exp1trains-table.txt: ns per store by the Pico's crystal (end mark minus start mark over 128), the same in CPU cycles at 33.8688 MHz, and Timer 2's cycles per store (two bracketing reads included: they agree with the Pico's to <0.4 cycle). Two earlier runs agree to 0.1 cycle.
The Pico's PIO overhead assumption (7 units an ACK) rides in the Pico's figure; the Timer 2 column does not and matches it, so the assumption is right.
Trains with 12 or more nops (3,300+ words, 24 nops 12 KB of code) do not fit the 4 KB I-cache: the slope past 8 nops (3.0 cycles a nop at 12 -> 16) includes instruction refills.
