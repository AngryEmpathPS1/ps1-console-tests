sio/sio1-loopback
SIO1 timed from the inside: rate, SR.0/SR.2, the start's alignment, and with a loopback plug
(4-1 TXD-RXD, 6-3 DTR-DSR, 8-5 RTS-CTS) the receiver, the FIFO and the interrupts. No assertions.

>>> NO BUTTONS IN THIS TEST - WATCH THE TV. When it turns BLUE: take the serial cable out of the console and put the loopback plug in. YELLOW: it is measuring (a second or two). GREEN: take the loopback plug out and put the serial cable back - no hurry: the results come 20 s after the plug is out.
THE PLUG: seen by its lines

LINES (SR.7 DSR, SR.8 CTS; each output on its own)
  off: DSR 0 CTS 0   DTR: DSR 1 CTS 0   RTS: DSR 0 CTS 1   both: DSR 1 CTS 1
  DSR follows DTR; CTS follows RTS - joined as they should be
  data comes back (TXD to RXD): yes

THE START'S ALIGNMENT (8N1 MUL16 BAUD 0024h, a bit is 576)
  write   45 after BAUD      SR.0   656 after the write (  701 after BAUD)
  write  157 after BAUD      SR.0   649 after the write (  806 after BAUD)
  write  301 after BAUD      SR.0   649 after the write (  950 after BAUD)
  write  445 after BAUD      SR.0   653 after the write ( 1098 after BAUD)
  write  733 after BAUD      SR.0   649 after the write ( 1382 after BAUD)

THE FIFO (8N1 MUL16 BAUD 0001h)
  nine bytes, none read: SR.1 1 SR.4 (overrun) 1; reads: 01 02 03 04 05 06 07 09 00 00 00 00  (SR.1 now 0)
  five, then a sixth: reads 11 12 13 14 15 16 16 16 00 00  (psx-spx: the sixth is stored thrice; after six reads it reads twice more, then 00h)
  six, then a 16-bit read a2a1 and a 32-bit read a5a4a3a2: 1 left (a 16-bit read takes one, a 32-bit four)

INTERRUPTS (I_STAT.8, polled; interrupts stay off)
  RX: I_STAT.8 1 SR.9 1; ack with a byte left: I_STAT.8 1; ack with none: I_STAT.8 0 SR.9 0
  TX IRQ while idle: I_STAT.8 1; DSR IRQ with DTR off 0, then DTR on 1 (meaningful only if LINES says DSR follows DTR)

THE BAUD TIMER (SR bits 11-31; 8N1 MUL16 BAUD 0100h: reloads with 2048)
    111 at 38 cycles after the BAUD write,    88 at 573
