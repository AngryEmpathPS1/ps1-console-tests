TYPE : 6 free button or flying-V form
PS-X Control PAD Driver  Ver 3.0

sio/sio1-loopback
SIO1 timed from the inside: rate, SR.0/SR.2, the start's alignment, and with a loopback plug
(4-1 TXD-RXD, 6-3 DTR-DSR, 8-5 RTS-CTS) the receiver, the FIFO and the interrupts. No assertions.

>>> NO BUTTONS IN THIS TEST - WATCH THE TV. When it turns BLUE: take the serial cable out of the console and put the loopback plug in. YELLOW: it is measuring (a second or two). GREEN: take the loopback plug out and put the serial cable back - no hurry: the results come 20 s after the plug is out.
THE PLUG: seen by its lines

LINES (SR.7 DSR, SR.8 CTS; each output on its own)
  off: DSR 0 CTS 0   DTR: DSR 1 CTS 0   RTS: DSR 0 CTS 1   both: DSR 1 CTS 1
  DSR follows DTR; CTS follows RTS - joined as they should be
  data comes back (TXD to RXD): yes

ONE BYTE: cycles from the TX_DATA write (Timer2, CPU clock)
  8N1 MUL16 BAUD 0024h       bit   576  SR.0    584  SR.2   6341  RX   6140 (5a)
  8N2 MUL16 BAUD 0024h       bit   576  SR.0    601  SR.2   6926  RX   6124 (5a)
  8N1.5 MUL16 BAUD 0024h     bit   576  SR.0    601  SR.2   6638  RX   6124 (5a)
  8O1 MUL16 BAUD 0024h       bit   576  SR.0    601  SR.2   6912  RX   6718 (5a)
  8E1 MUL16 BAUD 0024h       bit   576  SR.0    601  SR.2   6912  RX   6718 (5a)
  5N1 MUL16 BAUD 0024h       bit   576  SR.0    570  SR.2   4604  RX   4410 (1a)
  8N1 MUL1 BAUD 0100h        bit   256  SR.0    187  SR.2   2740  RX   3004 (5a)
  8N1 MUL1 BAUD 0101h (odd)  bit   256  SR.0    181  SR.2   2767  RX   3031 (5a)
  8N1 MUL64 BAUD 0010h       bit  1024  SR.0   1056  SR.2  11285  RX  10803 (5a)
  8N1 MUL16 BAUD 0001h       bit    16  SR.0     52  SR.2    182  RX    182 (5a)
  8N1 MUL16 BAUD 00D8h       bit  3456  SR.0   3401  SR.2  37958  RX  36644 (5a)

THE START'S ALIGNMENT (8N1 MUL16 BAUD 0024h, a bit is 576)
  write   47 after BAUD      SR.0   645 after the write (  692 after BAUD)
  write  167 after BAUD      SR.0   644 after the write (  811 after BAUD)
  write  311 after BAUD      SR.0   646 after the write (  957 after BAUD)
  write  455 after BAUD      SR.0   640 after the write ( 1095 after BAUD)
  write  743 after BAUD      SR.0   640 after the write ( 1383 after BAUD)

THE FIFO (8N1 MUL16 BAUD 0001h)
  nine bytes, none read: SR.1 1 SR.4 (overrun) 1; reads: 01 02 03 04 05 06 07 09 00 00 00 00  (SR.1 now 0)
  five, then a sixth: reads 11 12 13 14 15 16 16 16 00 00  (psx-spx: the sixth is stored thrice; after six reads it reads twice more, then 00h)
  six, then a 16-bit read a2a1 and a 32-bit read a5a4a3a2: 1 left (a 16-bit read takes one, a 32-bit four)

INTERRUPTS (I_STAT.8, polled; interrupts stay off)
  RX: I_STAT.8 1 SR.9 1; ack with a byte left: I_STAT.8 1; ack with none: I_STAT.8 0 SR.9 0
  TX IRQ while idle: I_STAT.8 1; DSR IRQ with DTR off 0, then DTR on 1 (meaningful only if LINES says DSR follows DTR)

THE BAUD TIMER (SR bits 11-31; 8N1 MUL16 BAUD 0100h: reloads with 2048)
    125 at 31 cycles after the BAUD write,   105 at 563

CTS AND THE TRANSMITTER (RTS drives CTS through the plug; 8N1 MUL16 BAUD 0024h, a bit is 576; one byte ~6,300)
  1. a byte written with CTS off: in 40,000 cycles SR.0 never SR.2 56 RX never
     then CTS on: SR.0 +622 SR.2 +6376 RX +6160 (a5)
  2. CTS off 1798 cycles after the write (3 bits into the byte): SR.2 at 6356, RX at 6156 (3c)
  3. second byte queued at 704, CTS off with it: RX 6142 (11), 11940 (22) - the second at +- after CTS on if it waited (then never 00)
