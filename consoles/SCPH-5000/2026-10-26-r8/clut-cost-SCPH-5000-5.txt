What does a CLUT cache RELOAD cost? gpu/clut-cache says WHEN the palette
cache is invalidated; nothing says what the reload is worth.
Two runs of 128 identical sprites: one CLUT throughout, then alternating
between two CLUT addresses so every sprite reloads. The difference is it.
