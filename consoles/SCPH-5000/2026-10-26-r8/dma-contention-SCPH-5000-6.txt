run three times, changing only what the CPU does while it is in flight.

  SPIN     14156 cycles   (CPU polls CHCR, touches no RAM - what dma/chopping does)
  REGS     14259 cycles       51 iterations   (CPU busy, 64 ALU ops per iteration)
  STORES   15606 cycles       18 iterations   (CPU busy, 64 word stores per iteration)

  LOADS    15407 cycles        4 iterations   (CPU busy, 64 lw/lw/sw steps per iteration)
  LOADS step during DMA:  60.18 cycles  (256 steps)
  LOADS step with NO DMA: 22.27 cycles  (4096 steps, 91221 cycles)

  STORES did 35% of the iterations REGS did.
  transfer time, STORES vs SPIN: +1450 cycles
  transfer time, REGS   vs SPIN: +103 cycles

  A FLAT WINDOW (this core's model) predicts all three transfer times
  equal and STORES/REGS explained only by the cost of a store.
  CONTENTION predicts STORES slower, fewer iterations, or both.
