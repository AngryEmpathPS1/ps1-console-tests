
cpu/io-store-shadow
A store in an on-die load's shadow (cached; Timer 2 cycles, fastest..slowest of 16 over the refresh).
F p: lhu T2; sh T1_TARGET at p; nops; lhu T2 at 9 - the total.
G p: lhu T2 MODE; sh 0,T2 at p; nops; lhu T2 at 9 - the count read (p 0: no load).
H p: lhu T2; sw RAM at p; nops; lhu T2 at 17 - the total.
There are no assertions - compare against your emulator.
