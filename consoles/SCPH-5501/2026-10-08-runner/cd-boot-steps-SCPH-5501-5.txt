
cdrom/cd-boot-steps
The drive's steps in a game's boot (ReadTOC and Init after a double-speed read), an Init while circling at double speed, and the holds a seek leaves.
Needs a pressed disc of 53+ minutes. Cycles at the system clock (1 ms = 33,869). About a minute; nothing prints until each part is done.
