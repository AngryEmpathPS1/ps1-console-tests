
cpu/ram-store-io
An I/O read behind RAM stores (cached; Timer 2 cycles, fastest..slowest of 16 over the refresh).
Z: two Timer 2 reads.  S n k: n stores to one 1 KB block, k nops.  P n k: alternating between two blocks.
G n: n alternating stores, a GPUSTAT read.  L n: n alternating stores, a RAM read.  N: 4 alternating, 4 nops.
There are no assertions - compare against your emulator.
