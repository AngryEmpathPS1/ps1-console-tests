
cpu/ram-banks
Four stores alternating X, Y, X+4, Y+4, then a RAM read (cached; Timer 2 cycles, fastest..slowest of 16).
X = A (2 KB aligned); Y = A + 512 (SAME), 1K .. 5K (ALTnK); the read from A+8, or A+5K+8 (ALT1K/Z5K).
PRE1K / NOPRE1K: a read from A+1K (or A+4K) first, four stores to A's block, the read from A+1K+8.
ONE: a store to A, the read from A+8. There are no assertions - compare against your emulator.
