
cpu/bus-latch
What fills the top half of a 32-bit read of I_MASK: each case's last word on the main bus.
There are no assertions - compare against your emulator, and say which console model ran it.

HOT (second call, lines cached)                    top    rules
  sw A1A1FFFFh to I_STAT                   0002   A1A1 it drives the bus (this core), 5A5A it does not
  sw A5A5xxxxh to I_MASK, read T2 target   A5A5   5A5A it does not (imask-upper; this core), A5A5 it does when another is read
  sh B2B2B3B3h to RAM+2                    8001   B3B3 the halfword shifted to its lane (this core), B2B2 the register
  sh B2B2B3B3h to RAM+0                    8001   B2B2 the register's own upper half (this core), 0000 lanes left undriven
  sb C4C5C6C7h to RAM+3                    8001   C700 the byte shifted to its lane (this core), C4C5 the register
  sb C4C5C6C7h to RAM+1                    3C05   C5C6 the byte shifted to its lane (this core), C4C5 the register
  marker, lw BFC00000h (ROM, 3C08xxxxh)    3C07   3C08 a ROM read drives it, 5A5A it does not (this core)
  marker, lw SPU voice 0 ADSR (E5E51111h)  3C05   E5E5 an SPU read drives it, 5A5A it does not (this core)
  sw D6D62222h to SPU voice 0 ADSR         0200   D6D6 an SPU store drives it (this core), 5A5A it does not
